Privacy

How this site handles information when you visit, write to us, or pick a language.

Last updated · 13 August 2026

Who we are

Tier Studios is an independent game studio. This notice covers tierstudios.com and related pages we publish. For privacy questions, write to info@tierstudios.com.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email, subject, and message you send. We use that only to reply. The site also stores your language choice in the browser so the interface stays in the language you picked.

Cookies

We use necessary storage for language and your cookie choice. Analytics cookies run only if you allow them in Cookie Settings. Details are on the cookie policy.

Third parties

The contact form is delivered through Formspree. Outbound links (Discord, Steam, Unity Asset Store, social accounts) are run by those services. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

You can change analytics cookies anytime from . You can also ask us to delete a message you sent, or clear site data in your browser.
