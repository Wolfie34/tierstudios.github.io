Cookies

What this site stores in your browser, and how you can change it.

Last updated · 13 August 2026

Necessary

Language (tier-lang) and your cookie choice (tier-cookies) are stored locally so the site remembers them. These are required for the site to work as you set it. They are not used for ads.

Analytics

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited. This is off until you turn it on in Cookie Settings. You can switch it off again at any time.

How to change

Open from the footer, or from this page. You can also clear site data in your browser. See also the privacy notice.
